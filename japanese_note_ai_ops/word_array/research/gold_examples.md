Notable things

- Just like above `<b>` tags are stripped
- Punctuation and other non-word text like html tags are in their own array alone.
- The `<k>` tag specifically is included inside words. This example shows the two edge cases:
  - `<k> 登[のぼ]り</k> きったら` is a whole word where the tag lands in the middle. The opening `<k>` can be outside the word the closing `</k>` can't, e.g. highlighting the whole word would incorrectly become `<k><b> 登[のぼ]り</k> きったら</b>`, this would be programmatically fix (if find`<k><b>`, switch to `<b><k>`). The inner highlight would be no problem, it's `<k><b> 登[のぼ]り</b></k> きったら` for 登る and `<k> 登[のぼ]り</b></k><b> きったら</b>` for 切る.
  - `<k> 見下[みお]ろせた</k>` both words wrapped with `<k>`. Simple case, `<b>` tags fall inside the `<k>` without
- Words that split into sub-words contain the same array structure in their sub_words array
